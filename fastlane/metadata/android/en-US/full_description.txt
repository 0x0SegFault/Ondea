<p>Ondea finds your fertile days from your basal body temperature. It is designed for people who are trying to conceive.</p>

<p><b>Easy daily tracking:</b> enter your waking temperature in a few seconds. You can also log period flow, cervical mucus, intercourse and notes.</p>

<p><b>Science-based:</b> Ondea confirms ovulation with the "3 over 6" temperature-shift rule of the symptothermal method. Until the shift is confirmed, it predicts your fertile window and peak days from your own past cycles.</p>

<ul>
<li>Temperature chart with coverline, fertile window and ovulation</li>
<li>Calendar of recorded and predicted periods and fertile days</li>
<li>Next period forecast and late-period notice</li>
<li>Start a new cycle or pause tracking at any time</li>
<li>Optional discreet daily reminder</li>
<li>°C and °F</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Private by design:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission, no account, no ads, no trackers</li>
<li>Works without Google Play Services</li>
<li>Data encrypted on the device with a key held by the Android Keystore</li>
<li>Optional app lock with fingerprint or screen lock; screenshots blocked</li>
<li>Password-encrypted backups that you can save anywhere, including Proton Drive</li>
</ul>

<p>Ondea is not a contraceptive and not a medical device.</p>

<p>Ondea is a period and ovulation tracker for people who are trying to conceive. It uses your morning (basal) body temperature and your cervical mucus to show your fertile days and to confirm ovulation.</p>

<p><b>Quick daily entry:</b> set your temperature to 0.01 °C with one wheel per digit, or type it. Log your period, cervical mucus, ovulation tests, intercourse and notes with one tap.</p>

<p><b>Learns day by day:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Before any sign, the forecast uses the ovulation day you set, or a typical cycle.</li>
<li>Fertile mucus or a first high temperature moves the estimate of the current cycle.</li>
<li>"Do your signs agree?" compares your temperature and your mucus, and asks you to check disturbed readings when they do not agree.</li>
<li>After a cycle, Ondea shows what your signs point to and suggests a new ovulation day. Only you change it.</li>
</ul>

<p><b>See your cycle:</b> cycle ring, temperature chart with phases and the typical curve, calendar, next period and chance of conception per day. A short tour explains how to measure and how to read the chart.</p>

<p><b>Based on published studies:</b> each rule and its source are listed in the app (Settings → Science and sources).</p>

<p><b>Private by design:</b></p>
<ul>
<li>No internet access, no account, no ads, no trackers</li>
<li>Works without Google Play Services</li>
<li>Data encrypted on the phone</li>
<li>Optional app lock with fingerprint, face or screen lock; screenshots blocked</li>
<li>Backups encrypted with your password; export to a spreadsheet (CSV)</li>
<li>English, French, German, Spanish and Italian; °C and °F</li>
</ul>

<p>Ondea is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. It is not a contraceptive. Consult a healthcare professional for medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.</p>
